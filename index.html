Questions

Why do you keep 5%?

Because getting help to people costs something: boxes, tape, postage, and hours of sorting and admin. We keep the smallest amount that makes the work possible and we don't spend it on anything else.

Are payment processing fees included in the 5%?

No — card and payment processor fees are taken before the money reaches us. Our 95/5 split applies to funds we actually receive.

Do you run your own programmes?

No. We are a pass-through. All of the funds we receive are transferred directly to vetted partner charities working on the ground. We publish a receipt for every transfer in the receipts/ folder of our public repository.

Who decides where the money goes?

The four-person team listed above. Our criteria are published, and every decision is listed publicly on this page.

Refund policy

We refund every penny that has not already been transferred to a partner charity, without question.

Because we operate as a pass-through, funds are batched and transferred to our partner charities on a monthly schedule. Once a batch has been transferred and the receipt is published on this site, that portion of a donation can no longer be refunded by us — but the partner charity's own refund policy may still apply, and we will help you contact them.

How to request a refund: email us with the date, amount, and the name the donation was made under. We aim to respond within 48 hours and to process approved refunds within 5 working days.

What counts as "claimed": any amount that appears in a published receipt in our receipts/ folder is considered transferred and no longer refundable by us. Everything else is refundable in full.
